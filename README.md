## About me

I'm a full-stack engineer who builds for the realities of the Nigerian market, where checkout runs on Paystack, customers live on WhatsApp, and every price is in Naira. My work centres on the systems that carry real money and real messages: multi-tenant SaaS, payment flows and messaging infrastructure.

What draws me in is the unglamorous engineering that decides whether a product survives real users: **signature-verified webhooks, role-based access control, and making sure the same ticket is never sold twice.**

I'm currently interning at **HNG**, building and shipping in a team under real deadlines. I'm open to **remote developer roles** and contract work in fintech, e-commerce and SaaS.

## Problems I've actually solved

- **Multi-tenant isolation:** role-based workspaces (admin, finance, inventory, messaging) where every sub-user's data is scoped to their admin.
- **WhatsApp Cloud API:** moved from tokens that expired every 24 hours to a permanent System User setup.
- **Payments you can trust:** Paystack checkout, refunds and payouts, with webhooks that are signature-verified and safe to receive twice.
- **Shipping with a team:** wrote and enforced a conventions doc so 10 people's code looked like one person's.
